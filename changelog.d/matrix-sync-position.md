- **A sleeping laptop no longer loses your messages.** The agent now keeps its Windy Chat sync position across restarts and sleeps, so when the computer wakes it answers what you wrote to it meanwhile in your chat with it, once. Before, anything older than two minutes was silently skipped. Every message is also handled at most once across restarts, so waking up never answers or acts on something twice. A message older than an hour is never acted on: the agent tells you once how many it saw and asks what you still want done. If the chat could not deliver everything from while it was away, it says so once. A file you sent while it was offline is not opened; it tells you once to send it again.
