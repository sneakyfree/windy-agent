- **One certifier, one birth canal.** `windy cert` and `/cert` are gone. They looked for a local certificate PDF that
  this program stopped making in 0.7.5, and told you to "run /go to generate" one. Your agent's birth certificate is
  issued by Eternitas at the Windy hatch ceremony, and nothing here makes another. The status table no longer shows a
  "Certificate: Pending" row that could never change, exports no longer pick up an old local PDF, and the unused PDF
  library is no longer installed. A file you already have is not touched.
