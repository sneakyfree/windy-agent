- **A real agent never marks its traffic as a test.** `X-Windy-Synthetic` (and `metadata.synthetic`) now come only from a test-born agent (ET26-TEST-*) or from a probe that has no passport at all. `WINDY_SYNTHETIC=1` is ignored when the process holds a real passport (from `ETERNITAS_PASSPORT` or its token's `sub`). Each request checks again, so a passport loaded after start-up is honoured. Windy Mind had seen Zero's real passport marked synthetic on about 165 calls a week.
