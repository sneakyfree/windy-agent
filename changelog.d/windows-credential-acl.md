- **On Windows, the agent's credentials are now readable by your account only.** The agent's key, your Windy sign-in and the bring-home files used to inherit their folder's permissions, which can let other administrators on the computer read them. They now get an owner-only permission list (the same as `chmod 600` on macOS and Linux), and the agent's state folder is owner-only too. Two of these writers also no longer crash on Windows.
