- **`/model` never confirms a switch Windy Mind will refuse.** Picking a model that isn't available right now used to say "Switched to ..." and then answer on another model. Now it says "I can't switch to <model>: <why, in plain words>. I'm still answering with <current model>." and the pick is left unchanged. The why comes from Mind's catalog `unavailable_reason`; a code Fly doesn't know reads "it isn't available right now". When Mind's catalog can be read, a family word or a shortcut (sonnet, smartest) only switches to a model Mind offers.
