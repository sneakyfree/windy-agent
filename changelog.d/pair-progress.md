- **`windy home pair` shows a number that moves through every long wait.** While the phone approves, it shows the seconds left. While Windy finishes the move, it says what it is waiting for (Windy Chat, Windy Mail) and the time left. After a minute it adds "this can take a few minutes". The memory's download shows the MB so far (and the % when the size is known), and saving it shows the %. `--json-status` sends these as `progress` lines for Windy Word's bar. A test fails if any long stage goes more than 10 s without a moving number. Nothing new is sent or stored.
