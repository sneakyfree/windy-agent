- **A person who texts STOP is never texted again.** Windy Fly's own texting channel now keeps a record when someone
  opts out, and refuses every later text to that number, on every path, until they text START. An opt-out is STOP,
  STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE or OPT OUT as the whole message (any case, spacing or punctuation),
  a short message with STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, OPT OUT or REVOKE in it ("stop please"), or a phrase such as "stop texting",
  "don't text" or "remove me". "Cancel my appointment" is still a request, and "yes" never re-subscribes anyone. Until
  the owner turns it on, the channel texts only the owner's own number.
- **HELP always gets an answer.** Anyone who texts HELP, including someone who opted out, gets one fixed reply: who the
  agent is, who it texts for, the support address, and how to opt out. It does not re-subscribe anyone.
