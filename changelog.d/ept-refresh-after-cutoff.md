- **After an Eternitas EPT cutoff, the agent renews its passport token through its owner's Windy sign-in.** When Eternitas refuses the agent's own token as issued before the passport's cutoff (`ept_before_cutoff`, ept-cutoff v1), the refresh tries the owner's stored Windy sign-in once instead of stopping.
