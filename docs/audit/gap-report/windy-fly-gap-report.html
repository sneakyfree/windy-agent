How to read the columns
  • Feature: what the strand says we are building, with the codon id and where it stands now.
  • Built? Yes, Partly or No, checked against master today. "Waiting" means built and tested in a PR that has not merged yet.
  • Visible? Can you see it working in the product today: on Zero, in Windy Chat or on a running gateway, not only in a test. Merged work turns Yes only after Zero is updated on Boss's GO.
  • Working: RED not built or not working · YELLOW partly works, fixed on master but not yet on Zero, or built and waiting · GREEN built, merged and proven by a test, or retired on the record with a reason. The small grey line shows the colour in Saturday's update.
