**Top 5 gaps:**
1. **Presets and tone sliders do nothing by default.** `raw_mode=1` makes buddy and engineer produce a byte-identical prompt, yet `/preset` says "applied" (control_panel.py:359, commands/core.py:1536). That is a silent no-op shown to the user.
2. **Matrix E2E is not actually on.** There is no `[e2e]`/olm, so `ENCRYPTION_ENABLED=False`; encrypted rooms can't be read, and the "E2E enabled" log line (matrix_bot.py:994) is false.
3. **Windy Pro tools are registered unconditionally with a localhost default and human-JWT-only auth** (windy_api.py:23-34). On passport agents they likely fail, against the 10-10 "register only when configured" rule.
4. **Soul import has no user-facing path.** It is reachable only through UDS/HTTP; "review-sensitive" isn't implemented, `needs_review` is never read, and the parsed personality is dropped on approval.
5. **Friction detection misses common corrections** ("That is wrong…", "Incorrect…", "Actually…", "that's not what I asked"). Separately, Matrix pending replies flush only on reconnect or shutdown, never on the next successful sync.
